CodeQuest – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Data we collect

CodeQuest does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your learning progress (completed lessons, stars, XP, streak and settings) is saved locally on your device only and is never sent to us. Code you type in the Playground and lesson exercises runs entirely on your device and is not uploaded.

In-app purchase

The optional "Full Version" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details or personal information.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/svashchenko8877/codequest/issues.
