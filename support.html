CodeQuest – Support

Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue at github.com/svashchenko8877/codequest/issues and include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. Found a mistake in a lesson? Tell us the lesson number (for example "swift-42") and we'll fix it.

Restore your purchase

Open the Profile tab and tap Restore purchase. You need to be signed in with the Apple ID that bought the Full Version.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
